In Poland, kilometrówka is the reimbursement for using a private car for work. The maximum rates are **PLN 1.15 per kilometre** for cars with an engine over 900 cm³ and **PLN 0.89 per kilometre** for cars up to 900 cm³. A business trip from Warsaw to Kraków ({{km warszawa-krakow}} km) gives **{{allowance warszawa-krakow 1.15}}** one way – while the fuel itself costs about {{trip warszawa-krakow pb 7}}. Amounts on this page are converted to euro.

## Mileage allowance rates

| Vehicle | Rate per km |
|---|---|
| Car, engine over 900 cm³ | PLN 1.15 |
| Car, engine up to 900 cm³ | PLN 0.89 |
| Motorcycle | PLN 0.69 |
| Moped | PLN 0.42 |

The rates have applied since January 2023 and are maximums – an employer may pay less, but reimbursement above them doesn't get the same tax treatment.

## Does the allowance cover the cost of the car?

At today's petrol price ({{price PL pb}}), a car using 7 l/100 km spends {{cost100 PL pb 7}} on fuel per 100 km, while the higher allowance rate pays PLN 115. The difference is meant to cover everything else: tyres, servicing, insurance and depreciation. With high fuel prices and a thirsty car that margin shrinks – so it's worth knowing your real cost per kilometre. More in [how much it costs to drive 100 km](guides/cost-per-100-km).

## How to claim it

- **Keep a mileage log** – record the date, purpose, route and distance. For a business trip (delegacja) the travel order is the basis.
- **The rate depends on engine size**, not on the fuel type.
- **Details depend on the situation** – business trips and local driving in the town where you work follow different rules. If in doubt, check with your accountant or HR department.
