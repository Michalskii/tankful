## How to pay less at Italian pumps

- **Use "fai da te", not "servito".** Many stations have self-service pumps (fai da te) and attended ones (servito). The same litre costs noticeably more with service.
- **Avoid motorway stations.** Prices on the autostrada are usually well above the national average – the next town is often cheaper.
- **LPG is easy in Italy.** Italy has one of the largest LPG station networks in Europe.
- **Night and Sunday machines.** Many stations run as self-service machines only at night and on Sundays – keep the receipt if the machine doesn't give change.

> At Italian pumps petrol is **Benzina** (often "senza piombo"), diesel is **Gasolio** and LPG is **GPL**.
