From **3 October to 31 December 2026** petrol (95) and diesel in Poland carry a reduced VAT rate – **8% instead of 23%**. LPG stays at the full rate. The average price of petrol is **{{price PL pb}}** today; at 23% VAT it would be **{{vatold pb}}**. Our [fuel cost calculator](en) applies the new rate automatically.

| Fuel | At 23% VAT | Now (8% VAT) | A full 50-litre tank is cheaper by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Petrol (95) | {{vatold pb}} | {{price PL pb}} | {{vatsave pb 50}} |
| Diesel | {{vatold on}} | {{price PL on}} | {{vatsave on 50}} |

A return trip doubles the saving. On trips abroad the cut only applies to the Polish part of the route – from Warsaw to Berlin it is about {{vattrip warszawa-berlin pb 7}}, because you buy the rest of the fuel in Germany anyway.

## Filling up in Poland from abroad

The cut makes Poland even more attractive for drivers from neighbouring countries: petrol now costs {{price PL pb}} in Poland against {{price DE pb}} in Germany and {{price CZ pb}} in Czechia. Which side of the border to fill up on is covered in [where to fill up near the Polish border](guides/refuel-before-border).

## Where the numbers come from

> The price at 23% VAT is today's average net price with the full tax rate. Pump prices also depend on wholesale prices and margins, so the actual difference at a given station may vary. Average prices come from the European Commission's Weekly Oil Bulletin, adjusted for changes in Orlen wholesale prices.
