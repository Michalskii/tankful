With a 20 km commute each way you drive about 840 km a month (21 working days, there and back). In a car that uses 7 l/100 km, petrol at Poland's average price comes to about **{{cost PL pb 7 840}} a month** today, and about **{{cost PL pb 7 9240}} a year** (11 working months). At German prices the same month costs about {{cost DE pb 7 840}}. With 40 km each way it is {{cost PL pb 7 1680}} a month at Polish prices.

A year is roughly eleven times as much – with 40 km each way, about {{cost PL pb 7 18480}} of petrol.

## And in an electric car?

An electric car using 17 kWh per 100 km and charged at home costs about **{{ev100 17 home}} per 100 km**, against {{cost100 PL pb 7}} for petrol at 7 l/100 km. A 20 km commute (840 km a month) takes about 143 kWh – at {{evprice home}} clearly less than {{cost PL pb 7 840}} of petrol. On fast chargers ({{ev100 17 fast}} per 100 km) most of the advantage disappears. We use typical electricity prices – your tariff may differ. More in [the cost of a trip by EV](guides/ev-trip-cost).

## Sharing the ride pays off

If a colleague drives the same way, the cost is split in half. With 40 km each way on petrol that is {{cost PL pb 3.5 1680}} instead of {{cost PL pb 7 1680}} a month each, and {{cost PL pb 2.333 1680}} with three people. How to split it fairly when someone rides a shorter distance is covered in [splitting fuel costs](guides/split-fuel-cost).

## What else changes the cost

- **Traffic jams** – rush-hour city driving can raise consumption by more than ten percent. Check your real consumption with the [fuel consumption calculator](guides/fuel-consumption-calculator).
- **Short trips in winter** – the engine takes a long time to warm up, so on short trips the car uses noticeably more.
- **Business trips in a private car** are claimed differently – Polish per-km rates are in [the Polish mileage allowance](guides/polish-mileage-allowance).

Work out your exact commute – from your home address to work – in the [calculator](en), then multiply by the number of working days.
