## Traffic, winter and consumption

- **The Zakopianka at weekends.** The last stretch to Zakopane, national road 47, can be jammed for hours on Fridays, Saturdays and during winter school holidays. Stop-and-go driving raises consumption, so leave early or travel midweek.
- **Cars use more in winter.** A cold engine, heating, winter tyres and climbs can add 10–20% to consumption – enter a higher figure in the calculator.
- **A roof box for skis** adds a few more percent at expressway speeds – see [how to reduce fuel consumption](guides/reduce-fuel-consumption).
- **Share the ride** – see [how to split fuel costs fairly](guides/split-fuel-cost).
