## Where to refuel on the way south

Slovakia is the shortest way from Poland to Hungary, to Croatia via Hungary and to the Slovak Tatras. On the Kraków – Budapest drive ({{km krakow-budapeszt}} km) the Slovak section is a large part of the trip:

{{route krakow-budapeszt}}

- If Poland is cheaper, **fill up before the border** – at Chyżne, Chochołów, Zwardoń or Barwinek. A full tank usually gets you to Budapest.
- If Slovakia is cheaper, refuel just after the border rather than at motorway stations, which are usually above the national average.
- On the way back, do the same in the other direction – the table above also compares Slovakia with Hungary.

## Fuel names and paying

> At Slovak stations 95 petrol is **Natural 95** (or **benzín 95**), diesel is **Nafta** or **Diesel**, and LPG is **LPG** or **autoplyn**. You pay in euro, by card almost everywhere.

## Slovak vignette

Slovak motorways and expressways need an electronic vignette. Buy it on the official eznamka.sk website – it is linked to your number plate, so there is no sticker. Short, multi-day and annual vignettes are available. Avoid reseller websites that charge more for the same vignette.
