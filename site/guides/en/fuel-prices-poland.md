## Where these prices come from

> National averages from the European Commission's Weekly Oil Bulletin. When Orlen wholesale prices clearly move after the bulletin date, we adjust the Polish petrol and diesel price by that change. Prices at individual stations differ from the average – often higher in cities and on motorways, lower at hypermarkets.
