## Fuel names, paying and the vignette

> In Slovenia 95 petrol is **NMB 95**, diesel is **Dizel** and LPG is **Avtoplin**. You pay in euro, by card almost everywhere.

Slovenian motorways and expressways need an electronic vignette (e-vinjeta), sold on the official DARS website and linked to your number plate. The Karawanken tunnel from Austria has a separate toll that the vignette does not cover.
