## How to use the calculator

- **Kilometres a year** – how far you drive in a year. This matters most: the more you drive, the faster the system pays off.
- **Petrol use** – your car's average consumption. If you don't know it, work it out with the [fuel consumption calculator](guides/fuel-consumption-calculator).
- **LPG use** – a car usually burns 15–25% more LPG than petrol. The calculator fills in 20% more, but you can change it.
- **Fuel prices** – Polish averages by default. Enter the prices at your station for a more accurate result.
- **Conversion cost** – a sequential system, fitted, usually costs €800–1,300 in Poland and more in Western Europe. Direct-injection engines cost noticeably more.
- **LPG servicing** – system inspections, filter changes and any extra roadworthiness tests. The calculator subtracts this from the yearly saving.
