## Vignettes in Slovakia and Hungary

| Country | What you need | Where to buy |
|---|---|---|
| Slovakia | electronic vignette for motorways and expressways | eznamka.sk |
| Hungary | electronic vignette (e-matrica), national or for selected counties | ematrica.nemzetiutdij.hu |
| Czechia (route from Warsaw) | electronic vignette | edalnice.cz |

All of them are linked to your number plate – nothing to stick on. A cheaper county vignette is enough if you only use the motorway in one or a few counties – check which ones your route crosses. Buy on the official sites; resellers add a fee.

## How to pay less

- **Fill up where it's cheapest** – at {{km krakow-budapeszt}} km, one full tank usually covers the drive one way.
- **Share the car** – split four ways, petrol from Kraków is {{trip krakow-budapeszt pb 7 4}} per person. See [how to split fuel costs fairly](guides/split-fuel-cost).
- **Avoid motorway stations** – towns along the way are usually cheaper.
