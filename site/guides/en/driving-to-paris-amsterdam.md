German prices change many times a day, and evenings are often cheapest – more in [fuel prices in Germany](guides/fuel-prices-germany).

## Road charges and city zones

| Country | Motorways | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Germany | free for cars | low emission zones (Umweltzone) in many cities – green sticker |
| Belgium | free for cars | tolled Liefkenshoek tunnel in Antwerp; low emission zones in large cities |
| Netherlands | free for cars | tolled Westerschelde and Kil tunnels; environmental zones for older diesels |
| France | most motorways tolled at booths (péage) | Crit'Air sticker in low emission zones, including Paris |

French motorway tolls depend on the stretch, so check them before you go – cards are accepted at the booths. Order the Crit'Air sticker on the official site, certificat-air.gouv.fr, well in advance, as it comes by post.

## How to pay less

- **Leave Poland with a full tank** – the first few hundred kilometres through Germany are where you save the most.
- **Ease off on the German autobahn** – on unrestricted stretches it's easy to drive fast, and above 130 km/h consumption climbs steeply. See [how to reduce fuel consumption](guides/reduce-fuel-consumption).
- **Split the cost** – over this distance fuel adds up, so it's worth [splitting it fairly](guides/split-fuel-cost).
