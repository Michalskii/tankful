## When to fill up in Austria

- **In the morning.** Austrian stations may raise prices only once a day, at noon, and lower them at any time – so before 12:00 is usually cheapest.
- **Check the official price finder.** Stations must report their prices to the regulator E-Control, and the free Spritpreisrechner (spritpreisrechner.at) lists the cheapest ones near you.
- **Leave the motorway.** Motorway stations charge noticeably more – a station in the next town is often over ten cents a litre cheaper.
- **Fill up before the border if it's cheaper.** On 50 litres of petrol the difference between Czechia and Austria is {{tank CZ AT pb 50}} today.

> At Austrian pumps petrol (95) is **Super**, diesel is **Diesel** and LPG is **Autogas**. Few stations sell LPG, so fill an LPG car up before you enter Austria.
