## There and back

A week by the sea means driving both ways, so double the fuel cost. From Kraków in a petrol car that's about **{{roundtrip krakow-gdansk pb 7}}** for the whole trip, from Warsaw **{{roundtrip warszawa-gdansk pb 7}}**. Add a few dozen kilometres for day trips while you're there.

## Motorway or expressway

From southern and central Poland the main tolled stretch is the A1 motorway between Toruń and Gdańsk. Expressways, including the S7 from Warsaw to Gdańsk, are free for cars. Toll rates change, so check them before you go.

## How to pay less for the drive

- **Share the car** – the fuel cost per person falls with every passenger. See [how to split fuel costs fairly](guides/split-fuel-cost).
- **Avoid peak days.** On summer Fridays and Saturdays, traffic on the S7 and A1 can add hours to the trip, and stop-and-go driving raises consumption.
- **Skip the roof box if you can.** At motorway speeds a roof box noticeably increases fuel use – more in [how to reduce fuel consumption](guides/reduce-fuel-consumption).
- **Fill up before the motorway.** Motorway stations are usually more expensive than those in towns.
