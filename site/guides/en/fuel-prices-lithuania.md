## Where to refuel

- From Poland most drivers take the S61 (Via Baltica) through the **Budzisko – Kalvarija** crossing or the **Ogrodniki – Lazdijai** crossing. The last large Polish stations are around Suwałki.
- If Poland is cheaper, fill up in Suwałki – a full tank covers Vilnius and the way back to the border.
- Stations on the main roads are often above the national average, in Lithuania too.

> In Lithuania 95 petrol is **Benzinas 95**, diesel is **Dyzelinas** and LPG is **Dujos** or **LPG**. You pay in euro, by card almost everywhere.
