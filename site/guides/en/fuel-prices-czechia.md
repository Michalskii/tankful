## Fuel names and the vignette

> At Czech stations, 95 petrol is **Natural 95**, diesel is **Nafta** (or Diesel) and LPG is **LPG** or **autoplyn**. Cards are accepted almost everywhere, though prices are shown in koruna.

Czech motorways and expressways require an electronic vignette. Buy it on the official site, edalnice.cz – it's linked to your number plate, so there's nothing to stick on the windscreen. Options range from a few days to a full year. Avoid reseller sites that charge more for the same vignette.

Roads without the vignette requirement are free but usually slower. Cost your own trip to Czechia and beyond in the [Tankful calculator](en), and compare prices across the EU in the [fuel prices in Europe](guides/fuel-prices-europe) table.
